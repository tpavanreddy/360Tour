Usage Instructions

Basic Navigation:

1. Open tour.html in your browser 2. The 360° viewer loads on the left 3. The 2D map appears on the right with scene markers 4. Click green dots on map to navigate between scenes 5. Use Auto Follow to keep map centered on current location

Loading GPX Tracks:

1. Click "Choose File" to upload your GPX file 2. Or click "Load Sample Track" for demo data 3. Track appears as colored line on map 4. Map auto-zooms to show entire track

360° Video Support:

1. Change "Media Type" dropdown to "360° Videos" 2. Place MP4 360° videos in panos directory 3. Update tour.xml with video scene configurations 4. Videos play with map synchronization
